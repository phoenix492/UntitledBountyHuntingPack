# Death World
## Impossible Planet 
- No liquid water to speak of. 
- No edible food. 
- Not really any natural resources either.
- This is for all intents and purposes an uninhabitable planet.
- And yet it's filled with murder-monsters.
- Implication: Monsters subsist entirely off cannibalizing the unedible flesh of each other. 
## The Corporation
- Your "benefactors"
- Willing to send you payment for services rendered in hunting monsters and sending the materials back
- Also willing to send you supplies, water, food, and essentials... in exchange for the payment they just gave you.
- Squeezing a profit here is near impossible.
- Also livestreaming your entire struggle as a syndicated broadcast pay-per view show.
- It's honestly quite the business model
  - They're making cash hand over fist on the margins they mark up things to you
  - And on the top dollar prices all the parts you send them for pennies fetch.
  - And on this being one of the top-rated shows in the entire system despite being pay-per-view.

## Your Difficulties
- It's blisteringly hot. You overheat to death if god or the weather looks at you wrong.
- You need to drink the non-existent water on the planet.
- You need to eat the non-existent food on the planet.
- Nearly everything wants to kill you.
- You're contractually bound here for the foreseeable future. 

## The Truth
- They didn't just "find" this planet like this
- Crazy illegal experimentation was happening here on how to produce these rare parts en-masse.
- Maybe even something else greater.
- Mutations, risky potential-black-hole spawning matter replication.
- They were violating just about every ethical standard on the books. 
- And every trademark and patent standard.
- All while carrying far more equipment, protection, and supplies than you'll ever have.
  - Plenty of that is still there, you know!
- Exploring abandoned company branded bunkers causes them to cut to commercial break 
  - It's clear they're hiding something.
- It also seems to really piss them off
  - Which isn't typically a good idea when they're your one (subpar) lifeline for supplies.
- They glassed/bionuked the entire planet when it became clear that they'd be found out.
- Winning the pack involves finding survivors and gaining access to a final bunker, where the corpo attempts to kill you directly.
- Once you beat (FINAL BOSS), you'll find where they finished a major breakthrough, intergalactic transport technology, right before the planet was bio-nuked as a coverup.
  - Interstellar was possible before, just at speeds similar to that of boats in our world. True teleportation and especially to that of unvisited galaxies was the major breakthrough everything has been leading to for civilization. 
- Their crimes will be exposed, you'll be hailed as a hero, and the universe and civilizaion will enter a new age.
